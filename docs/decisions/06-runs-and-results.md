## Suite attempts and completeness

How the suite reports, per platform, what it attempts and how well it does it (project owner decision, 2026-11-20). Design and prototype only: `src/glance/rollup.ts`, `scripts/rollup-prototype.ts`, `scripts/suite-attempt-prototype.ts` and their tests; not yet part of `npm run regen`. The approach is written up in [docs/34](../34-suite-attempts.md).

- <a id="d385"></a>**Scope-relative headline (D385, agreed).** Each platform surface gets one primary figure P, the conformance-implied agreement on the measured part of its scope: agrees / (agrees + differs + unexplained), item level, gating (registry-channel) checks only. It is always printed with the measured share M, the scope size and the floor F, and withheld as "insufficient evidence" when M is under 50% or fewer than 10 items were decided. Advisory checks give a labelled second line, never P. Ceremony completion is a separate figure by kind of partner and is never a conformance count. Where most of a scope has no gating check, the gap is closed by promoting reviewed latest-version vectors into the registry in batches, not by counting advisory checks or lowering the floor.
- <a id="d386"></a>**What a suite is (D386, agreed).** A suite is a task-force family (first slug segment) at the latest versions. A family with more than 30 focus tasks is split by its second segment; after a split, sub-prefixes with fewer than 3 tasks are pooled as `<family> (other)`. The framework rule groups and the five framework tasks form `core`. Each ceremony group is listed with the suites whose tasks it names, beside them, never inside their figures. Today: 112 suites from 862 focus tasks.
- <a id="d387"></a>**Scope from code evidence (D387, agreed).** A suite is attempted when the platform includes any of its tasks: served in a dispatcher table or handler, sent by a builder (comment-stripped), or declared by the adapter at the measured build. Names without a builder or handler (constants, allow-lists, pins) never count as implemented. Each attempted suite shows its evidence kinds and a confidence: high when code and the adapter declaration agree, medium with one of them. Tasks found in code but not declared by the measured build are in scope as not measured.
- <a id="d388"></a>**Completeness and conformance (D388, agreed).** Per attempted suite two numbers: completeness (tasks implemented at the latest version / focus tasks in the suite, older-only tasks listed beside it) and conformance (P on the implemented tasks, with its measured share). A partial implementer is scored only on what it implements. A suite's conformance is printed as a percentage when at least half its implemented tasks were measured and at least 3 decided; otherwise "insufficient evidence" or "no measurement" with the counts. The headline pools the suites' items, so per-suite figures add up to it.
- <a id="d389"></a>**Three bands (D389, agreed).** Attempts (scored), not attempted (no role by design; listed, never counted against the platform) and not yet built (could take part, nothing found; shown, not scored). A suite with no evidence and no role reading is shown as not yet classified, with the missing input named. Role readings from judgement rules are labelled as judgement.
- <a id="d390"></a>**Override file optional (D390, agreed).** `results/scope/<component>.json` (`tt-scope-override/0.1`) may classify a suite without evidence or annotate an attempted one (for example a feature compiled out of the default build). It never removes a suite that has evidence and never adds an attempt; disagreements show both readings; suites classified by an override are counted as such in the headline. Each entry carries its basis: declared (who confirmed it and when), inferred (with an evidence link) or unconfirmed.
- <a id="d391"></a>**VTI derived line and surfaces (D391, agreed).** VTI is shown as one derived line, labelled derived, that names its weakest surface, and broken out by surface (agent service, community service, client builders, witness service, further services as measured). The surfaces are never silently merged into one figure. An overlap view shows the suites VTI and keyring-wallet both attempt, where one sends and the other serves, and the ceremony groups both took part in: conformance-implied and ran-together evidence, not demonstrated interoperation.
